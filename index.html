GAME GUIDE

ABOUT COZY ONET

Cozy Onet is a relaxed tile-matching game where you clear colorful pairs by connecting identical icons with a short, unobstructed path.

How to Play

  1. Choose one of the eight board movement levels and start a game.
  2. Choose a board size for a shorter or longer round. The size locks when you continue with Next Level.
  3. In portrait orientation, each board size swaps its columns and rows automatically.
  4. Select two matching icons. Their path may make up to two right-angle turns and can travel around the board edge.
  5. Use Hint to reveal a possible pair, or Shuffle to change the icons in place when you need a fresh layout.
  6. Clear every pair to finish the level. Music starts with the game and can be adjusted or muted at any time.

Scoring

  • Unassisted match: +20
  • Manual or automatic shuffle: −10
  • Per-level time bonus depends on board size: Very Easy +4,000, Easy +5,500, Medium +7,000, Hard +8,500, and Very Hard +10,000, minus 10 per second.
  • Level completion bonus depends on board size: Very Easy +200, Easy +400, Medium +600, Hard +800, and Very Hard +1,000.
  • A pair shown by Hint does not earn match points.
  • Your final score carries into the next level only when you use the Next Level button. Replaying a level resets its score to that level's starting score.
  • Leaderboard scores are available only after completing levels 1–8 in order using Next Level; the board size is recorded too.

Each level adds its board-size time bonus, match points, shuffle penalties, and board-size completion bonus to the carried score.

Shortcuts

  • SShuffle the board
  • MToggle background music
